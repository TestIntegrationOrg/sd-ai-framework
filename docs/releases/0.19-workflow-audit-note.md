# 0.19 Workflow Audit Boundary

The 0.19 workflow audit surface records deterministic, hash-bound provenance for workflow, policy, constitution, state, artifacts, and existing AI execution evidence.

It does **not** provide enterprise identity verification. Existing local approval artifacts may be referenced by hash as governance evidence, but approver names and roles remain local assertions until the deferred 0.18 identity-backed approval capability is explicitly resumed.

The audit ledger is evidence only. Workflow state, policy evaluation, approval decisions, validation, promotion, contract checks, architecture drift, and other deterministic engines remain authoritative for their own domains.
